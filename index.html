Common questions

The block screen doesn't open the quiz.

Toll uses a notification to open the quiz. Make sure notifications for Toll are on: Settings app > Notifications > Toll.

My apps aren't being blocked.

Check that Toll still has Screen Time access: Settings app > Screen Time > Apps with Screen Time Access. Then open Toll; it asks again if access was removed.

How do I stop Toll from blocking apps?

Change or delete your blocked times in Toll's Settings tab. To remove Toll completely, delete the app; its blocks are removed with it.

How do I cancel Toll Pro?

Toll Pro is a subscription through Apple. Cancel it in the Settings app: tap your name > Subscriptions > Toll. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Toll Pro but it isn't active.

Open Toll, go to Settings > Toll Pro and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Toll see what I do on my phone?

No. Toll uses Apple's Screen Time framework and never sees your app usage or browsing. Everything Toll stores stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
