Toll

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

The short version: Toll has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you set up and do in Toll stays on your iPhone. If you buy Toll Pro, only anonymous purchase information leaves your device, so your subscription can be checked.

What Toll stores on your iPhone

  • Your settings: app groups, blocked times, quiz settings and topics.
  • Your activity in Toll: quiz answers, streaks, minutes bank and stats.

This information is saved only on your device, in storage shared between Toll and its own extensions. It isn't sent to us or anyone else. Deleting Toll deletes it.

Screen Time

Toll uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps you pick. Apple gives Toll a private reference for each app you choose, not your app usage, browsing history or what you do inside other apps. Toll can't see that information and doesn't collect it.

Notifications

Toll's notifications are created on your iPhone itself, for example to open the quiz from a block screen or to remind you that a blocked time is about to start. They aren't sent through any server.

Toll Pro purchases

Toll Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

To check whether Toll Pro is active, Toll uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app and your purchase information (which plan, when it was bought, and whether it is active). It doesn't receive your name, email or what you do in Toll. This information is used to unlock Pro and to show us anonymous sales figures, and isn't used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Toll doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
